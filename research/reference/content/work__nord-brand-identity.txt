[y=1575 fs=52px] The
[y=1562 fs=52px] brief
[y=1545 fs=17px] Nord Coffee roasted great beans in an industrial unit and sold them in plain brown bags. Cafés liked the coffee and forgot the name.
[y=1621 fs=17px] They wanted an identity small enough for a two-person roastery and strong enough to sit on a café shelf next to the big brands.

[y=1821 fs=52px] What I
[y=1808 fs=52px] did

[y=1876 fs=17px] Drew a wordmark from the shapes on the roaster's control panel
[y=1943 fs=17px] Built a bag system where colour marks the origin and type marks the roast
[y=2009 fs=17px] Designed a one-page website and a wholesale sheet for cafés
[y=2075 fs=17px] Prepared print files and a short brand guide the founders can use alone
