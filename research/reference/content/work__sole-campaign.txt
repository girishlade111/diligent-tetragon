[y=1498 fs=52px] The
[y=1485 fs=52px] brief
[y=1469 fs=17px] Sole Supply had a sneaker drop in six weeks and a website that still looked like a reseller's catalogue. They needed a launch that felt like a brand, not a sale.
[y=1544 fs=17px] We built one campaign page, one product template and a checkout that never leaves the story, then tested every step with real buyers before the drop.
[y=1715 fs=12px] SC 02
[y=1744 fs=52px] What I
[y=1731 fs=52px] did
[y=1734 fs=17px] Ran a two-day kick-off with the Sole Supply team to agree the one thing launch week had to prove
[y=1829 fs=17px] Art-directed a two-day shoot around a single red sneaker and a yellow wall
[y=1895 fs=17px] Designed the campaign page and product template in Figma as one system
[y=1962 fs=17px] Built the front end in React with the in-house developer, reviewed every Friday
[y=2028 fs=17px] Stayed on for launch week to watch the numbers and fix what slowed people down
