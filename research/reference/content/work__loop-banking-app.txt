[y=1486 fs=12px] SC 01
[y=1516 fs=52px] The
[y=1503 fs=52px] brief
[y=1486 fs=17px] Loop Bank had a licence, a backend and a beta app that people opened once. Signing up took eleven screens and most people stopped at the ID check.
[y=1562 fs=17px] I led product design for the app from private beta to public launch, and built the prototype the team tested every week.
[y=1732 fs=12px] SC 02
[y=1762 fs=52px] What I
[y=1749 fs=52px] did
[y=1751 fs=17px] Mapped every step of sign-up and cut it from eleven screens to five
[y=1817 fs=17px] Rebuilt the ID check so it explains what happens and why, in plain words
[y=1884 fs=17px] Designed the home screen around the one number people actually check
[y=1950 fs=17px] Prototyped in code so we could test real flows with real people every week
[y=2016 fs=17px] Led a team of four designers and ran a weekly critique with engineering
