[y=1245 fs=32px] Start with what already exists
[y=1321 fs=17px] The system I built for a team of 40 engineers began as an audit, not a library. We printed every button in the product. There were 31 of them.
[y=1441 fs=32px] Four rules
[y=1502 fs=17px] Tokens before components.
[y=1500 fs=17px] Colour, type and spacing first, so everything else has something to stand on.
[y=1570 fs=17px] Only what is used twice.
[y=1568 fs=17px] A component joins the system when two teams need it, not before.
[y=1610 fs=17px] Names a developer can guess.
[y=1608 fs=17px] If you have to look a token up, it has the wrong name.
[y=1649 fs=17px] One owner.
[y=1647 fs=17px] Someone answers for the system every week, or nobody does.
[y=1699 fs=31.68px] A design system is a product. Its users are your own team.
[y=1859 fs=32px] What it took
[y=1936 fs=17px] Six months, one designer and one engineer part-time. The button count went from 31 to four, and new screens now take days instead of weeks.
