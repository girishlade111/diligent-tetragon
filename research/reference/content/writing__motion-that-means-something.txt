[y=1245 fs=32px] Three questions
[y=1321 fs=17px] Before anything moves on screen, I ask three things:
[y=1351 fs=17px] What changed, and does the person need to notice?
[y=1390 fs=17px] Where did it come from, and where is it going?
[y=1429 fs=17px] Would it be clearer if it did not move at all?
[y=1519 fs=32px] Motion as explanation
[y=1595 fs=17px] Good motion answers a question the person was about to ask: where did that panel go, what did I just delete, is it still loading. Motion that answers nothing is decoration, and decoration gets tiring by the third visit.
[y=1706 fs=31.68px] If you can't say why it moves, it shouldn't.
[y=1828 fs=32px] Keep it short
[y=1904 fs=17px] Most interface motion should finish in under 300 milliseconds. Anything longer makes people wait for the interface instead of the other way round.
