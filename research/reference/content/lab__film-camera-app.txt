[y=1066 fs=32px] The idea
[y=1136 fs=17px] Phone cameras made photos instant and, for me, forgettable. I wanted to design a camera that brings the wait back.
[y=1210 fs=17px] You get 24 shots per roll, no preview, and the photos develop one day after you finish the roll.
[y=1309 fs=32px] How it's built
[y=1380 fs=17px] A SwiftUI concept with a real capture pipeline: photos are stored encrypted until the roll is done, then a background task develops them with a grain and colour profile per film stock.
[y=1438 fs=17px] Three film stocks, each with its own colour curve
[y=1475 fs=17px] No screen preview, only the frame counter
[y=1511 fs=17px] Development runs overnight as a background task
[y=1548 fs=17px] Designed in Figma, prototyped in SwiftUI
