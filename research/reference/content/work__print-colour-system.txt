[y=1528 fs=12px] SC 01
[y=1557 fs=52px] The
[y=1544 fs=52px] brief
[y=1528 fs=17px] Studio Chroma prints art books and runs a website, and the two never matched. Every reprint started with a week of colour arguments.
[y=1603 fs=17px] They asked for one palette that behaves the same on screen and on paper, documented so their freelancers could use it without calling them.
[y=1774 fs=12px] SC 02
[y=1803 fs=52px] What I
[y=1790 fs=52px] did
[y=1793 fs=17px] Printed a test sheet of their current colours on three paper stocks
[y=1859 fs=17px] Rebuilt the palette in a perceptual colour space so steps look even
[y=1925 fs=17px] Matched every screen colour to a print recipe and checked it on press
[y=1991 fs=17px] Built a small web tool that shows each colour on screen next to its print values
[y=2058 fs=17px] Wrote a short guide with the do's and don'ts for designers and printers
