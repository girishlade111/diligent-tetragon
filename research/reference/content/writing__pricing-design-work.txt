[y=434 fs=19px] Day rates, fixed scopes and why I stopped quoting by the screen.

[y=1268 fs=32px] Why not by the day
[y=1344 fs=17px] Day rates reward slow work and punish clear thinking. Clients also hate them, because the number they care about — the total — is the one nobody can tell them.
[y=1464 fs=32px] Fixed scope, fixed price
[y=1540 fs=17px] After a first call I write a one-page proposal: what we will make, what we will not, how long it takes and what it costs. If the scope changes, the price changes, in writing, before the work.
[y=1598 fs=17px] A one-week sprint to start, priced on its own
[y=1638 fs=17px] Then a fixed quote for the build
[y=1677 fs=17px] Payment in three parts, the last one on launch
[y=1767 fs=32px] Why not by the screen
[y=1843 fs=17px] Screens are a bad unit. A settings page and an onboarding flow are both 'one screen' and differ by weeks of work.
[y=1925 fs=31.68px] Price the result, not the hours and not the pixels.
[y=2108 fs=12px] Call sheet · Open
[y=2139 fs=40px] Need this for your product?
[y=2197 fs=16px] I design and build digital products end to end — from the first sketch to production code. Tell me what you’re making.
