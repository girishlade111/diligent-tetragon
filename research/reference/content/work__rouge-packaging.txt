[y=1545 fs=12px] SC 01
[y=1575 fs=52px] The
[y=1562 fs=52px] brief
[y=1545 fs=17px] Maison Rouge sells lipstick in 40 shades and their customers kept ordering the wrong one. The packaging was beautiful, the online choice was guesswork.
[y=1621 fs=17px] The job: new packaging that makes the shade obvious, and a 3D configurator that lets people see the case and the colour together before they buy.
[y=1791 fs=12px] SC 02
[y=1821 fs=52px] What I
[y=1808 fs=52px] did
[y=1810 fs=17px] Audited returns with the customer team to find which shades caused the most mistakes
[y=1876 fs=17px] Designed a packaging system where the shade is printed on the cap, not hidden underneath
[y=1943 fs=17px] Modelled the case in Blender and built a lightweight 3D viewer for the web
[y=2009 fs=17px] Tested the configurator with twelve customers on their own phones
[y=2075 fs=17px] Handed over the 3D files and viewer code to the agency running the shop
