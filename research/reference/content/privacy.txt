[y=450 fs=32px] Who I am
[y=526 fs=17px] This website is the portfolio of Mara Voss, an independent product designer and developer based in Berlin. I am responsible for the personal data collected through this site, and you can reach me at any time at the email address at the end of this page.
[y=690 fs=32px] What I collect
[y=765 fs=17px] I only collect what I need to answer you and to keep the site running well.
[y=814 fs=17px] Contact form:
[y=812 fs=17px] your name, email address, company, website, budget, timeline and your message. Only your name, email and message are required.
[y=882 fs=17px] Email:
[y=880 fs=17px] if you write to me directly, I keep your address and our conversation.
[y=921 fs=17px] Usage data:
[y=919 fs=17px] anonymous statistics about page views, referring sites, device type and country, collected by a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies or follow you across other sites.
[y=1104 fs=32px] Why I use it
[y=1179 fs=17px] To reply to your enquiry and, if we decide to work together, to prepare a proposal and a contract.
[y=1219 fs=17px] To see which pages are useful, so I can improve the site.
[y=1258 fs=17px] To meet legal duties such as bookkeeping and tax records.
[y=1324 fs=17px] The legal bases are your consent, steps taken at your request before a contract, and my legitimate interest in running a secure and useful website.
[y=1460 fs=32px] Cookies
[y=1535 fs=17px] This site only uses cookies that are strictly needed for it to work, for example to remember that you closed a notice. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking pixels. If an embedded video or booking tool wants to set its own cookies, it will ask for your permission first.
[y=1699 fs=32px] Who I share it with
[y=1775 fs=17px] I never sell your data or share it for marketing. A few trusted services process it on my behalf: the website host, the form and email provider and the analytics tool. Each one is bound by a data processing agreement and may only use your data to provide its service. When a provider is based outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by standard contractual clauses.
[y=1968 fs=32px] How long I keep it
[y=2044 fs=17px] Enquiries that do not become a project: deleted after 12 months.
[y=2083 fs=17px] Client correspondence and invoices: kept as long as the law requires, which in Germany can be up to 10 years.
[y=2151 fs=17px] Analytics: kept in aggregated form only, never linked to you.
[y=2278 fs=32px] Your rights
[y=2353 fs=17px] You can ask me at any time to show you the personal data I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how I use it, or to send you a copy in a portable format. Where I rely on your consent, you can withdraw it whenever you like. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
[y=2547 fs=32px] Security
[y=2622 fs=17px] The site is served over HTTPS, my inbox and tools are protected with two-factor authentication, and I keep the data I hold to the minimum I need.
[y=2757 fs=32px] Changes to this policy
[y=2832 fs=17px] If I change how I handle your data, I will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes stay highlighted here for at least 30 days.
[y=2999 fs=12px] Questions?
[y=3032 fs=40px] Write to me and I’ll answer within a day, in plain words.
